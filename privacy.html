Privacy policy

Effective October 13, 2026.

Purpose and access

Homebridge Nest is a personal integration operated by the account owner. It uses Google authorization to access selected Nest device information, home and room metadata, live video and audio, snapshots, and camera and doorbell events, according to the permissions the owner grants. It also requests Google Cloud Pub/Sub access so its plugin can receive event messages.

Use and sharing

Data is used to display and operate the authorized doorbell in Apple Home and deliver its notifications. Device data passes between Google's services, the owner's Homebridge computer, and the owner's Apple Home devices. If the owner enables Apple recording features, their storage and processing are governed by the owner's Apple settings and Apple's policies.

This personal integration does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising, unrelated analytics, or training generalized AI models. Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials and refresh tokens are stored in an owner-restricted Homebridge configuration on the owner's computer. Device metadata may be cached locally, and operational logs may contain device names, events, and errors. Google Cloud may retain queued event messages according to the subscription's settings. Any locally created backups may also contain credentials and configuration.

The owner controls local configuration, caches, logs, and backups and can delete them. No camera data or credentials are published on this information website.

Revoking access

The owner can stop the integration, revoke its permissions through Google Account or Nest Partner Connections settings, and remove its local credentials. Revoking access stops future authorized Google access; it does not automatically erase previously stored local data or third-party recordings.

This website

This static website has no login, forms, analytics scripts, or embedded camera feed. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process ordinary request information, such as IP addresses, under GitHub's privacy statement. Google and Apple handle their services under their own privacy policies.

Contact and changes

For questions, contact the operator through the yashprince123 GitHub profile. Do not include credentials, private video, or home details in public communications. This policy will be updated if the integration's data practices change.
