Homebridge Nest

A private smart-home integration that connects an authorized Google Nest doorbell to Apple Home through Homebridge running on the owner's computer.

What the integration does

  • Provides live doorbell video and audio in Apple Home.
  • Receives camera and doorbell events through Google Cloud Pub/Sub.
  • Supports snapshots and Apple Home notifications where available.

The owner chooses which Nest devices and permissions to authorize through Google. Access is used only for this personal home integration.

This website provides application information only. It does not provide access to the home, camera video, Homebridge server, or Google credentials.
